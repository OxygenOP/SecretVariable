# Code of conduct

We want SecretVariable to be a welcoming project. Everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests, discussions and any other project space, agrees to:

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion on the work, not on people.
- Not harass, discriminate against or demean anyone.
- Not publish others' private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this, and may ban people who repeatedly or seriously do. Report concerns by contacting the maintainers through the repository's private security/contact channels. This code is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
